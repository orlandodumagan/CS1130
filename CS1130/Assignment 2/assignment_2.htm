Assignment #2 - Income Tax Calculator

Computes the income tax from an annual taxable income.

Uses the Philippine annual income tax table for individuals (2023 onward): 0% up to 250,000; 15% over 250,000; 22,500 + 20% over 400,000; 102,500 + 25% over 800,000; 402,500 + 30% over 2,000,000; 2,202,500 + 35% over 8,000,000. Replace the table in assignment2.js if your instructor gave a different one.
